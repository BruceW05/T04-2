Why energy consumption matters

Choosing an energy-efficient TV

Placeholder content: larger screens and higher refresh rates generally consume more power. OLED panels can be efficient when displaying darker content but may draw more power on bright scenes, while LED/QLED panels tend to have more consistent draw regardless of content. Checking the Energy Rating Label — and the estimated annual running cost figure it displays — is the quickest way to compare televisions before buying.
